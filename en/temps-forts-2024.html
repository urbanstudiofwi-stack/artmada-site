ARTMADA #1 · 5 → 15 Dec 2024

2024 highlights

The first edition: ten days across three towns, 14 murals, an exhibition, a debate and an open mic. Every evening, a video told the story of the day.

On this page : The launch · The debate at the TOM · The exhibition at the Territorial Archives · The hip-hop Open Mic · Cascade Citron and the stairs mural · The Floréal jam and the end of the festival

Start of the festival

The launch

Théâtre municipal Aimé Césaire, Fort-de-France

Launch night of the first edition, then the artists from Martinique, Guadeloupe, Togo and South Africa start on their walls.

See the Olympia mural

The debate at the TOM

TOM, rue Victor Sévère, Fort-de-France

A debate open to everyone: “Making Martinique a hub of Caribbean street art”.

Cascade Citron and the stairs mural

Cascade, Trénelle-Citron, Fort-de-France

A Friday evening of participatory workshops at Cascade Citron. In this neighbourhood, the Cascade stairs mural was painted with secondary and high school students.

The Floréal jam and the end of the festival

Ancienne école Les Roses, Floréal, Fort-de-France

A jam at the former Les Roses school, and the first mural tour by electric cart with Doudou Style. Then the first edition comes to an end.
