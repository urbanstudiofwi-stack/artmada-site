Tours & workshops

The murals stay on the walls all year round. Three ways to discover them, and one way to pick up a spray can yourself.

Self-guided walk

The murals of editions #1 and #2 are spread across Fort-de-France: Canal Alaric, Pointe Simon, rue Victor Sévère, Perrinon, Clarac, Ozanam… Take the map and walk at your own pace.

Price
Free
Duration
1.5 to 3 hours, on foot
Open the map

Guided tour

A member of the collective tells you the story of the walls: the artists, the techniques, the stories behind each mural. For schools, associations, companies and tourist groups.

Price
€250 per group
Duration
2 hours
Group
Up to 25 people
Request a date

Graffiti workshop

Try it on a real wall, then take home your own painted panel. With the artist Doudou Style, at the Urban Studio gallery.

Price
€49.90 all included
When
Saturdays, 10am–12pm
Places
8
Where
Urban Studio, Village de la Poterie, Les Trois-Îlets
Book on Bizouk

Group, school, company, birthday: the workshop is also available on request. Write to us at contact@artmadastreetfestival.com.
