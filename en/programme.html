To give you an idea: edition #2

The edition #3 programme will be published here as soon as it is ready. To give you an idea, here is how edition #2 unfolded, from 1 to 11 February 2026.

ARTMADA #2 · February 2026

2Feb3Feb4Feb5Feb6Feb7Feb8Feb9Feb10Feb11Feb

Before the festival20–22 Jan 2026

  • 20 → 22 Jan

    Graffiti jam with the Martinican artists

    Parking de la SOAME, Pointe Simon

    The Martinican artists paint one big wall together, open to the public.

    Murals
  • 20 Jan · 6–11pm

    Evening with the artists + screening of « Au pied du mur »

    Le Bayou, Fort-de-France

    Screening of Boucan Production's documentary about street art in Martinique.

    Night

Monday 2 FebKick-off

  • All day

    The murals begin

    Canal Alaric, Pointe Simon, rue Victor Sévère, Perrinon, Clarac…

    The artists start on their walls. Come and watch them paint until 11 February.

    Murals
  • 6–11pm

    Opening night

    La Terrasse FWI, Fort-de-France

    Festival launch: DJ set, breakdance show, partners and press.

    Night

Tuesday 3 FebMurals in progress

  • All day

    Live painting

    Fort-de-France

    The artists paint. Four RTM buses get ARTMADA colours.

    Murals

Wednesday 4 FebCustomise your sneakers

  • 2.30–5.30pm

    Sneaker customisation workshop

    Le Bocal, Fort-de-France

    Customise your shoes with street art techniques. Sold out.

    Workshop

Thursday 5 FebExhibition opening

  • 6–10pm

    « ArtMada 2 » exhibition opening and graffiti talk

    Le Bocal, rue Garnier Pagès, Fort-de-France

    The Mada Paint collective's exhibition, and a talk about graffiti in Martinique.

    Exhibition

Friday 6 FebOpen Mic

  • 6–11pm

    ARTMADA Open Mic

    Le 33, rue Perrinon, Fort-de-France

    Open stage for rap, slam, singing and poetry. DJ Jee on the decks, live painting, Soundjata showcase, food truck.

    Night

Saturday 7 FebWorkshop day

  • 10am–12pm

    Street art initiation with Doudou Style

    Écomusée de Martinique, Rivière-Pilote · Free

    Free workshop, registration required.

    Workshop
  • Morning

    Painting workshops « Lumière mensongère » and « Flow »

    Centre culturel Antoine Tangamen

    Painting under monochromatic light, then abstract painting, with Ludivine Rhino.

    Workshop
  • 1.30–3.30pm

    Breakdance and street art workshops

    TOM, rue Victor Sévère, Fort-de-France · Free

    Your choice: breakdance or street art. Free, registration required.

    Workshop

Sunday 8 FebMurals in progress

  • All day

    Live painting

    Fort-de-France

    The walls take shape. A good day to walk the route.

    Murals

Monday 9 FebMural tour

  • 3pm · 4pm · 5pm

    Mural tour by electric cart

    Fort-de-France

    Guided tour in small groups, booking required. Every departure sold out.

    Tour

Tuesday 10 FebFinal touches

  • All day

    Live painting

    Fort-de-France

    The artists finish their walls.

    Murals
  • 6pm–12am

    Pre-closing party

    Le Bayou, Fort-de-France

    One more night to keep the energy going before the finale.

    Night

Wednesday 11 FebFinale: block party

  • Daytime

    Mural tour by electric cart

    Fort-de-France

    Second day of guided tours, booking required.

    Tour
  • 2–4pm

    Street art and breakdance workshops

    Parking du TOM, rue Victor Sévère · Free

    The last workshops, open to all. Free.

    Workshop
  • 4pm–12am

    Closing block party

    Parking du TOM, rue Victor Sévère, Fort-de-France

    Papa Tank, Soundjata, DJ Kopp, DJ Jee and guests. Breakdance show, graffiti jam, bar, food truck. 1,500 people in 2026.
