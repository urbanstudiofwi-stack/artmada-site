ARTMADA #2

“Lyannaj Karayib” · the ties between the Caribbean islands

Eleven days of murals across Fort-de-France's neighbourhoods, with artists from Martinique, Guadeloupe, Haiti, Réunion, England and France. A festival-branded bus, an exhibition, an open mic, free workshops and a closing block party.

26murals
1 750 m²painted
23artists, 6 countries
75volunteers
1 500at the block party
1,2 Monline views

Highlights

  • The 20–22 January jam: Martinican artists paint the SOAME façade together at Pointe Simon
  • Four RTM buses wrapped in ARTMADA colours by Xän
  • « ArtMada 2 » exhibition opening and graffiti talk at Le Bocal
  • Hip-hop open mic at Le 33, rue Perrinon
  • Participatory mural at Cité Clarac with residents and Victor Anicet high school
  • Closing block party on rue Victor Sévère, with Papa Tank and Soundjata

The artists

Xän, Oshea, Magiic, Mounlan, Aurélie Argyre, Doudou Style, Rman, James, Onine, None, Saveur Grafikk, Roxane Shade, Jaddict, Zében, Curtis Hylton, Wuna, Rayza, Al Pacman, Yelow, Veneno, Tempo Nok, Box Vincent, Rdom

ARTMADA #1

Caribbean identity and Afro-descendance

The first edition: ten days across three towns. 14 murals: 11 in Fort-de-France, 1 in Saint-Pierre and 2 in Les Trois-Îlets. Artists from Martinique, Guadeloupe, Togo and South Africa, an exhibition at the Territorial Archives, a debate, an open mic and the first mural tour by electric cart.

14murals
900 m²painted
3towns
20volunteers

Highlights

  • Launch night at the Aimé Césaire municipal theatre
  • Debate “Making Martinique a hub of Caribbean street art” at the TOM
  • ARTMADA #1 exhibition at the Territorial Archives
  • Cascade stairs mural with secondary and high school students
  • Jam at the former Les Roses school, Floréal
  • First mural tour by electric cart, with Doudou Style

The artists

Xän, Oshea, Magiic, Mounlan, Aurélie Argyre, Breeze Yoko, Sitou, Steek, Kaldéa, Carole B., Shadem, Klēlo, Socabohm, Queen of Crabs

Au pied du mur

A 52-minute documentary by Lucie Margueritte and Stève Siracuse, produced by Boucan Production. Over three years, the camera follows the Mada Paint collective (Rman, James, Oshea, Xän, Onine, Magiic, Doudou Style) up to a huge mural on Fort-de-France's Malecon promenade, and to the birth of ARTMADA.

Broadcast on Martinique la 1ère on 20 January 2026, then on France Télévisions on 16 September 2026.
