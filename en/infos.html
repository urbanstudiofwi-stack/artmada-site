FAQ

Practical info

Does it cost anything?

No. The murals are free to see, in the street, during and after the festival. The festival's free workshops require registration. Only the Saturday workshop at Urban Studio (€49.90) and group guided tours (€250) are paid.

Where can I see the murals?

In Fort-de-France: Canal Alaric, Pointe Simon, rue Victor Sévère, Perrinon, Clarac, Ozanam… and, for edition #3, Langellier, Mongérald and Tocade. Everything is on the map.

Can I watch the artists paint?

Yes, that's the whole point! During the festival, the artists paint during the day. Stay behind the safety barriers and feel free to chat with them during their breaks.

How do I get there?

City-centre walls are best on foot. For the neighbourhoods, RTM buses and the BHNS rapid bus stop close by. Avoid driving in the city centre on party nights.

I'm an artist, how can I take part?

Artists are selected by the Mada Paint collective. Send your portfolio and Instagram to contact@artmadastreetfestival.com: we look at everything.

I have a wall to offer

Write to us with the address, a photo and the wall's dimensions. For a mural outside the festival, Urban Studio can also give you a quote.

Can I use the photos?

For personal use, yes, credit the artist. For press or commercial use, ask first: each mural belongs to its artist.
