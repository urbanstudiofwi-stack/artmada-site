Volunteers

We make it together

75 volunteers brought ARTMADA #2 to life. For edition #3, we need hands, smiles and drivers again. Even a few hours make a difference.

Welcome

Welcome visitors, give directions, answer questions about the walls.

Logistics

Prepare equipment, help on the walls, tidy up at the end of the day.

Driver

Drive the artists between their lodging, the walls and the events.

Photo & video

Capture the festival, day after day.

Events

Bar, entrance and set-up during the parties and the block party.

Workshops

Support participants alongside the artists.

How to sign up

Send us an email with your name, phone number, your availability between 25 January and 4 February 2027 and the missions you'd like. We'll get back to you before the festival.

  • No painting skills needed.
  • Festival T-shirt included, meals during your shifts.
  • Coming soon: sign up directly in the ARTMADA app.
