Rman

Magiic & Rman · ARTMADA #2 · Photo: Shaman

Co-founder of the Mada Paint crew in the 1990s, Rman is one of the pioneers of graffiti in Martinique. His work is rooted in graffiti and hip-hop culture, with a strong Caribbean identity.
