Curtis Hylton

Curtis Hylton · ARTMADA #2 · Photo: Shaman

An English muralist and street artist, Curtis Hylton brings nature and wildlife to life in large mural compositions. Inspired by natural species and driven by a strong graphic sense, his murals engage with architecture and public space, particularly in Europe.
