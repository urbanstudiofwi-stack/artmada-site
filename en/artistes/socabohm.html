Socabohm

Socabohm · ARTMADA #1 · Photo: vue drone, décembre 2024 · © ARTMADA / NPL MADA PAINT

Selected by the ARTMADA #1 jury, Socabohm questions and celebrates our visual heritage: the images we have created and those that were created of us. During the festival he also took a middle-school class on a sketch tour of Fort-de-France.
