Doudou Style

Doudou Style · ARTMADA #2 · Photo: Shaman

A graffiti artist from the Mada Paint crew, Doudou Style is known for a colourful universe blending graffiti, trompe-l'œil and mural painting. Founder of Urban Studio, Martinique's first urban art gallery, she exhibits locally and internationally. At ARTMADA #1 she ran workshops and led the mural tours.
