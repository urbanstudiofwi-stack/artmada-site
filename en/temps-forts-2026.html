ARTMADA #2 · 20 Jan → 11 Feb 2026

2026 highlights

Eleven days of murals across Fort-de-France's neighbourhoods, after a jam in January. Here are the moments that made ARTMADA #2, with the videos shot by J.King during the festival.

On this page : The Jam · Opening night · The exhibition opening at Le Bocal · The Open Mic · The night at Le Bayou · The closing Block Party

20 → 22 Jan 2026

The Jam

Parking de la SOAME, Pointe Simon, Fort-de-France

Before the festival, the Martinican artists paint one big wall together, open to the public. Three days of painting, meetings and energy: a taste of the festival.

Opening night

La Terrasse FWI, Fort-de-France

On the day the artists start on their walls, the festival is launched in the evening: DJ set, breakdance show, partners and press. Around the same days, four RTM buses get ARTMADA colours.

The exhibition opening at Le Bocal

Le Bocal, rue Garnier Pagès, Fort-de-France

Opening of the « ArtMada 2 » exhibition and a graffiti talk, with music and live painting.

The Open Mic

Le 33, rue Perrinon, Fort-de-France

A hip-hop open stage: the island's talents take the mic, after a first edition that left its mark in 2024.

The night at Le Bayou

Le Bayou, Fort-de-France

The night before the closing, the pre-closing party at Le Bayou, with music by Makandja.

The closing Block Party

Parking du TOM, rue Victor Sévère, Fort-de-France

We block the street and turn the sound up: street art and breakdance workshops in the afternoon, then the party until midnight with Papa Tank and Soundjata, at the foot of the TOM murals. 1,500 people came.

  • Participatory mural at Cité Clarac with residents and Victor Anicet high school
  • Street art, breakdance and painting workshops on 7 February (Écomusée de Martinique, Antoine Tangamen cultural centre, TOM)
  • Mural tours by electric cart on 9 and 11 February

The festival online

Over the 60 days around the festival (25 Dec 2025 → 22 Feb 2026), ARTMADA's Instagram account had 930,269 views, 65.6% of them from people who didn't follow the account yet. The account grew to 5,046 followers.

Source : ARTMADA 2026 communication report
