ARTMADA Street Festival

25 Jan › 4 Feb 2027

Edition #3 · Fort-de-France, Martinique

The theme of edition #3

An Lot Chimen

“In a time of social, military, climate and digital fatalism, is another way possible?”

For 11 days, artists from Martinique, the Caribbean and beyond paint the walls of Fort-de-France, live, in front of residents. Workshops, DJ nights, block party: the city changes its face.

Theme text: Xän, for the Mada Paint collective. All about edition #3
