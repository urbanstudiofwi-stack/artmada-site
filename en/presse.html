At a glance

ARTMADA Street Festival is the first festival in Martinique entirely devoted to street art. A free, open-air gallery for everyone, in Fort-de-France.

Monumental murals turn Fort-de-France into an open-air gallery. The festival is run by the Mada Paint collective (NPL MADA PAINT association), with the City of Fort-de-France.

Through every wall, performance and encounter, ARTMADA celebrates creation, cultural pride and passing on knowledge, with one simple aim: to make street art a positive, visible and unifying language in public space.

The murals stay: they turn the city into a living cultural trail, seen every day by residents and visitors alike. Around the walls: free workshops, a block party, exhibition openings, talks, performances, and a guided mural tour.

ARTMADA #3 “An Lot Chimen” (Another way) takes place from 25 January to 4 February 2027 in Langellier, Mongérald, Tocade and Fort-de-France city centre.
