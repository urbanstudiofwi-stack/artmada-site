ARTMADA #1 · 2024

Kaldéa

Photo © ARTMADA / NPL MADA PAINT

A Paris-based artist of Martinican origin, Kaldéa develops an original, luminous and deep chromatic body of work. Her universe is steeped in Asian, ethnic, fantasy and Art Deco influences.
