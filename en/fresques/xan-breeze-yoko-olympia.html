ARTMADA #1 · 2024

Xän & Breeze Yoko

Photo © ARTMADA / NPL MADA PAINT

Unmasked (Démasquée)

A four-handed mural: two women remove their masks, a nod to the Commedia dell'arte, about image, appearance and identity in the age of social media.

A versatile artist from the Mada Paint crew, Xän is known for murals of faces, Afro-Caribbean figures and symbolic motifs. He is drawn to heritage and describes his art as a universal language in constant change. He also designs ARTMADA's graphic identity.

A Cape Town-based multidisciplinary visual artist, Breeze Yoko works in graffiti, urban art and video and has painted in over 13 countries. Founder and artistic director of the Ubuhle Bendalo residency, he creates whimsical yet powerful murals that seek new ways to "read the city".
