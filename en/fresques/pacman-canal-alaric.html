The story of the mural

The last mural of edition #2, by Al Pacman. Born in Dominica and long based in Guadeloupe, where he revives neighbourhoods and runs the Just Graff It festival in Marie-Galante, he painted this wall in just three days.

In his grey-toned style, a gentle scene: the child is the new generation and the passing on of history; the mother is protection and love, today's diverse and creative Caribbean, her grip a sign of rootedness, her closed eyes of inner strength. Traditional-style tunics recall the bond with Africa, and lush flowers the beauty of Caribbean landscapes.

Text : Digital Street Art (Creapolis trail commissioned by ARTMADA)

The artist

Al Pacman

A self-taught graffiti writer, Al Pacman has been an influential figure of Caribbean graffiti since the 1990s. His monumental murals can be seen on several Caribbean islands and abroad. He also organises collective projects to beautify public spaces.
