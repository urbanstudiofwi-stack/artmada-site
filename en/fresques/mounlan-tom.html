The artist

Mounlan

A multidisciplinary artist (Mounlan èk lanmou irtisen), Mounlan painted his first mural during ARTMADA's first edition. Street art gives him a "kat kwazé", a crossroads where his voice meets the flow and energy that keep the city alive; there he pays tribute to those who are gone.
